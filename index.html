Minerality

Character in the glass.

The composition complements the menu, supports a preferred sensory profile, or expresses attributes selected for the event.

Quiet with a delicate course. Fuller when the plate asks for it. The glass should meet the menu.

Ca²⁺

Calcium

The backbone of body and mouthfeel. More calcium gives the glass weight beside a substantial course.

Mg²⁺

Magnesium

A gentle brightness that lifts the palate. Useful beside white wine and lighter courses.

HCO₃⁻

Bicarbonate

Softens acidity and rounds the palate. A useful character for food-forward, seated meals.

Si

Silica

Softness and slip on the palate, the quality that makes a glass feel finished.

Na⁺

Sodium

A precise touch of salinity, when the menu is coastal and the glass needs dimension.

pH

pH & Balance

How the other minerals read. Balance is set for the menu.

Minerality, the signature of a water

We name the glass by character. A quiet minerality stays out of the way of delicate food. A fuller minerality holds its own beside a richer plate.

The profile is matched to the menu and the day, composed by a certified water sommelier.

Advanced. TDS, if you want the measure

Total dissolved solids is the laboratory sum behind these characters. It is useful in the studio and secondary at the table. These ranges are a studio guide.

  • Ethereal, under 50 mg/L
  • Delicate, about 50 to 250 mg/L
  • Classic, about 250 to 800 mg/L
  • Rich, about 800 to 1,500 mg/L
  • Bold, above 1,500 mg/L
